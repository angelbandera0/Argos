Tablas: `pedidos` (status draft/open/in_review/fulfilled/returned/cancelled, buyer_id, currency_id, return_note), `pedido_lineas` (dest warehouse/store/direct, qty/unit/content en presentación de compra, qty_bought, qty_received), `pedido_linea_tiendas` (kind plan = desglose, received = reparto), `pedido_costos` (amount_given, spent, leftover R-11b, shortage_value) y `pedido_linea_costos` (unit_cost, faltante valorado) protegidas con admin/`see_costs`, `pedido_lotes`, `pedido_ajustes`.
